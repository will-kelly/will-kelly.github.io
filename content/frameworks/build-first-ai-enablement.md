description: "A practical method for turning AI strategy and training into governed, usable workflows."

**Build-First AI Enablement** begins with a bounded, meaningful use case instead of a lecture, tool rollout, or transformation promise.

The principle is straightforward: **build first, learn from actual use, and standardize what works.**

## The operating path

**Working use case → Interaction → Understanding → Modification → Broader capability**

1. **Diagnose:** Observe real work, identify friction, and record the current baseline.
2. **Bound:** Choose a small, valuable use case with a named owner and clear limits.
3. **Design and prototype:** Connect approved inputs, actions, and human review points.
4. **Validate:** Test output quality, data access, source traceability, and failure handling.
5. **Pilot:** Put the workflow in front of its intended users.
6. **Operationalize and improve:** Document the responsibilities, support path, measures, and scaling criteria.

## Guardrails that matter

Define what the AI may do, where a person must intervene, how exceptions are escalated, and when to stop or roll back. Governance belongs in the workflow, not in a slide deck beside it.

## Evidence of value

Look at completed tasks, reviewer effort, error rates, time saved *after* checking, user adoption, maintenance cost, and actual business results.

**Status:** Implementation methodology. Any specific pilot or production outcome requires separate verification.
