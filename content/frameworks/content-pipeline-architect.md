**Content Pipeline Architect** is a design model for the controlled path from business need to trusted content outcome. It is not simply an automated content generator.

## The nine-stage operating model

1. **Signal:** Identify the customer, product, field, or business need.
2. **Intake:** Record purpose, audience, owner, and priority.
3. **Framing:** Define the decision, deliverable, and acceptance criteria.
4. **Knowledge capture:** Gather approved claims, evidence, and subject-matter expertise.
5. **Production:** Create or transform material, using bounded AI assistance where appropriate.
6. **Review and validation:** Check accuracy, technical claims, voice, and source integrity.
7. **Approval:** Record explicit human editorial and specialist signoff.
8. **Distribution and activation:** Publish and adapt approved material for the intended channels.
9. **Measurement and renewal:** Review performance, reuse, correction needs, and expiration.

## Design rules

Every stage has an owner, inputs, outputs, quality conditions, and exception path. Content should be reusable across briefs, articles, white papers, one-sheets, and supporting sales materials without silently changing the meaning of the approved source.

## What to measure

Handoff delays, source coverage, review cycles, correction rates, reuse, maintenance effort, and downstream task or revenue influence where attribution is defensible.

**Status:** Pipeline operating-model specification. A diagram or prototype does not establish end-to-end deployment.
