A strategy becomes useful when it changes how work gets done. These frameworks focus on the operational layer: evidence, ownership, workflow design, human review, and measurable outcomes.

They are **methods and operating models**, not claims that every component is deployed in production.

## Explore the frameworks

- [Thoughtful Velocity](thoughtful-velocity/): Accelerate content operations without losing judgment, evidence, quality, or learning.
- [Build-First AI Enablement](build-first-ai-enablement/): Move from AI promises to a working, bounded use case and a repeatable capability.
- [Retrieval-Ready Enterprise](retrieval-ready-enterprise/): Make knowledge trustworthy, findable, current, and usable by people and AI.
- [Content Pipeline Architect](content-pipeline-architect/): Design the governed route from business need through evidence, creation, approval, activation, and renewal.
- [Content Systems ROI](content-systems-roi/): Measure the value of reusable content infrastructure, not simply asset production.

The common test: **What actually changed, who owns it, and what evidence shows it works?**
