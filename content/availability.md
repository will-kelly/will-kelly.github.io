I am interested in opportunities where technical content, enterprise knowledge, AI enablement, and go-to-market execution need to work as a connected system.

My priority is the **right full-time role**. I am also open to discussing substantive contract, fractional, or consulting engagements when the scope is clear and the work fits my experience.

## Full-time opportunities

I am especially interested in senior individual-contributor or strategic roles focused on:

- **AI enablement and adoption:** Turning AI tools and experiments into accountable workflows people can maintain.
- **Content strategy and operations:** Connecting editorial standards, trusted sources, production, review, distribution, and reuse.
- **Enterprise knowledge systems:** Improving how organizations capture, maintain, retrieve, and apply expertise.
- **Technical product marketing:** Positioning and communicating cloud, DevOps, DevSecOps, security, enterprise software, and AI products.

I bring a background in technical writing, B2B technology content, product marketing, and working across engineers, solution architects, subject-matter experts, and business teams.

## Work arrangements

**Remote opportunities are preferred.** I will consider a reasonable hybrid arrangement in Northern Virginia or the Washington, DC area for a strong fit.

The right opportunity matters more than a fashionable title. I am looking for a role with meaningful ownership, access to the people doing the work, and a practical mandate to improve the system rather than just increase output.

## Contract, fractional, and consulting work

For a well-defined project, I can help with technical content strategy, editorial and knowledge workflows, AI-assisted content operations, product narratives, technical research, thought leadership, and enabling teams to use trustworthy source material.

A useful engagement begins with the operating problem, existing constraints, decision ownership, and a tangible deliverable. I prefer a bounded starting scope over an open-ended transformation pitch.

## How I work

I connect strategy with implementation: clarify the audience and business problem, inspect existing sources and handoffs, design a useful workflow, test it with the people who own it, and measure what changes.
